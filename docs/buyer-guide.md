# Lean Sporty — Student / Buyer Guide

Knowledge base for the help assistant (buyer audience) and a human reference.
Every claim here must match what's built — when the product changes, update
this file. Instructor-facing answers live in `instructor-guide.md`.

---

## What Lean Sporty is

Lean Sporty is a dance and fitness platform for women. You can join **live
classes** taught by real instructors, buy **on-demand programs** (sets of
video lessons), and take the **21-Day Dance Challenge**. Everything runs in
your web browser — on a phone, tablet, or computer. No software to install.

## Signing in

- Sign in with **Google**, **Apple**, or an **email sign-in link** (we email
  you a link — there are no passwords to remember).
- If the email link doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check spam, or
  try signing in with Google or Apple instead.

## Live classes

- Browse upcoming classes on the **Live Classes** page. Some are free; paid
  class seats start at €5.
- To join a paid class, buy a seat with your card — checkout takes a minute
  and you get a receipt by email. Free classes just need a signed-in account.
- At class time, open the class page and watch live in your browser.
- **Replay:** if you were enrolled, you can rewatch the recording for
  **7 days** after the class. It usually appears within a few hours (up to a
  day) after the class ends.

## Programs

- A program is a set of video lessons you buy once — a simple list or a
  day-by-day plan. Many have a **free preview lesson** so you can try before
  buying.
- After purchase you get **12 months of access** from a one-time payment. No
  subscription, nothing recurring.
- Videos play in your browser and are protected — they can't be downloaded.

## The 21-Day Dance Challenge

- A 21-day dance program: **Day 1 is free** — try the full first workout
  before paying anything.
- The full challenge is a **one-time €49** purchase with 12 months of access.
- After buying, your workouts live in **My Program** — it remembers where you
  left off.

## Payments, receipts, and taxes

- Payments are by card, processed securely by **Stripe** — we never see or
  store your full card details.
- Prices are in **euros** and include VAT (sales tax). If your bank account is
  in another currency, your bank converts automatically at its own rate.
- You get a receipt by email after every purchase.

## Refunds

- **The challenge:** Day 1 is free precisely so you can decide before paying —
  because of that, the €49 purchase is not refundable. Try Day 1 first.
- **Other purchases:** contact us via the contact form — we handle refund
  requests case by case and always honor applicable consumer law.

## Problems watching a video

- Refresh the page first — that fixes most playback issues.
- Check your internet connection; video needs a stable connection.
- Try another browser (Chrome or Safari work best) or another device.
- Make sure you're signed in with the same account you bought with.
- Still stuck? Contact us — include what you were trying to watch and what
  you saw on the screen.

## Your account

- Manage your profile and settings from the account menu after signing in.
- You can delete your account yourself from the Settings page — or contact us
  and we'll take care of it.

## Want to teach on Lean Sporty?

- Lean Sporty is invite-only for instructors. Start at
  **leansporty.com/teach** — it explains the whole deal and has a short
  application form (your name, where you teach, a few lines about you).
- Instructors keep **80% of every sale after VAT** (featured instructors 85%),
  with no listing fee and no monthly fee. Everything is run for you: your own
  page, live streaming from the browser, checkout, receipts, and support.
- Applications are read personally — you'll hear back within a few days.

## Contact

For anything this guide doesn't answer: use the contact form at
**leansporty.com/contact**. A real person reads every message — usually the
founder — and we typically reply within a day.
